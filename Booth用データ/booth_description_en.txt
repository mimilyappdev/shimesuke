See your deadlines. Meet them with SchedulEX.

Juggling multiple projects and losing track of which deadline
is most urgent?

To-do apps don't handle deadlines well.
Calendar apps weren't built for project phase management.

SchedulEX is a browser-based project & deadline manager
built for anyone who has deadlines to meet.

---

■ Features

[Deadline Calendar]
All your projects displayed as color-coded phase bars
on a monthly calendar. See your busiest weeks at a glance.

[Reverse Scheduling]
Enter a deadline and SchedulEX automatically calculates
the start date for each phase. No more guessing when to begin.

[Urgency Colors]
Cards change color based on days remaining.
Your priorities jump out at you the moment you open the app.

[Templates]
Save phase durations by project type.
Next time, set up a new project in one tap.

[Color Customization ⭐ PRO]
Customize header, background, and card colors.
Choose from presets or set your own.

[Stamp Decoration ⭐ PRO]
Place stamps freely on your calendar.
Add unlimited custom stamps.
